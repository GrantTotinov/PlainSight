# English (en)

**Title** (75 max): PlainSight: Hide AI Overviews & AI Mode for Google Search™

**Short description** (132 max): Remove AI Overviews and AI Mode from Google Search. Get classic web results back. Free, open source, no tracking.

**Full description:**

Get your search results back, without an AI summary on top.

PlainSight removes AI Overviews and the AI Mode buttons from Google Search, so the first thing you see is a list of real websites again.

WHAT YOU GET
• A clean results page. The AI summary is hidden before it appears, so nothing jumps around.
• No AI Mode tabs or buttons on the results page or the homepage.
• Optional: send AI Mode back to regular results.
• Web only mode: every search opens in Google's own Web filter. Just links, and it keeps working even when Google redesigns its page.
• Pause on any tab with one click when you want the full page.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick a mode when you install. Hide AI keeps the normal results page and removes the AI parts. Web only switches your searches to the built-in Web filter, the most reliable option because it does not depend on the page layout. Switch modes or turn PlainSight off from the toolbar button at any time.

PRIVACY
PlainSight collects no data. No analytics, no tracking, no remote code, no network requests of its own. Your settings stay in your browser. The code is open source on GitHub, so anyone can check it.

Works on every Google domain, in any language.
